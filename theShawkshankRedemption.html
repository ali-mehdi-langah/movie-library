Movie Name: The Shawshank Redemption

Description

A wrongly imprisoned man finds hope, friendship, and strength while spending years in prison.

Movie Details

  • Story: A banker is imprisoned for a crime he says he did not commit and builds an unexpected friendship while seeking freedom.
  • Genre: Drama
  • Director: Frank Darabont
  • Main Actors: Tim Robbins, Morgan Freeman, Bob Gunton
  • Runtime: 2 hours 22 minutes
  • Famous For: Its powerful story, friendship, hope, and memorable ending.
